# Privacy Policy for PageClock

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026

PageClock is designed as an **offline-first**, privacy-respecting reading focus tracker. This policy outlines how your data is handled in strict compliance with the **Google Play Developer Policy** and **Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1 - Privacy)**.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
- **No Personal Data Collected:** PageClock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information (including names, emails, contacts, location, or device identifiers).
- **No Third-Party Trackers or SDKs:** PageClock contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or tracking libraries.

## 2. On-Device Storage (Offline-First)
- All reading sessions, subjects, notes, and metrics are stored strictly on your local device using SQLite.
- Your reading logs never leave your device unless you explicitly export them via the CSV export tool.

## 3. Data Ownership & Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)
- In accordance with GDPR and Apple/Google data control guidelines, users have complete ownership of their data.
- You can export all your data at any time (Settings → Export Sessions).
- You can permanently purge and delete all local records instantly at any time (Settings → Delete All Data).

## 4. Permissions
PageClock requests only the minimum necessary system permissions to function:
- **Foreground Service / Notification (Android):** To display an ongoing reading stopwatch status and keep accurate session tracking while your screen is off.
